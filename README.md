# LADE
Lightweight Architecture for Detection of Energy Depletion Attacks in LoRaWAN end devices

The Long Range Wide Area Network (LoRaWAN) is a widely adopted Internet of Things (IoT) technology in the industrial, agricultural, and academic sectors, providing low-power devices with excellent cost-effectiveness. With its strong adoption, security concerns with LoRaWAN are growing every day. In this context, a specific type of attack known as Energy Depletion Attack (EDA) aims to drain the battery of end devices until they become unavailable, posing a potential threat to the entire network and increasing the maintenance costs of the infrastructure.

In this context, LADE proposes and implements a detection method based on statistical distance metrics to identify anomalies in energy consumption of sensors. In this repository we have published the prototipe of LADE developed for two environments:

- **LADE for ContikiOS**: A prototype implemented for ContikiOS version 3. The prototipe can be executed in Cooja simulator.
- **LADE for x86/64 PC**: A prototype to evaluate the system into personal computers.


## Publication references:

- PROTO, André; MIERS, Charles C.; CARVALHO, Tereza CMB. Classification and characterization of LoRaWAN energy depletion attacks: A Review, published in IEEE Sensors Journal, 2024. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/JSEN.2024.3504259
  This work investigated the potential of the most common LoRaWAN attacks to deplete the energy of end devices. It presented a classification of these attacks and proposed a method to characterize their potential to deplete the energy of end devices. It then reviewed the most recent countermeasures against those attacks capable of depleting the energy of sensors.
  
- PROTO, André; MIERS, Charles C.; CARVALHO, Tereza CMB. An Intrusion Detection Architecture Based on the Energy Consumption of Sensors Against Energy Depletion Attacks in LoRaWAN, published in the International Conference on Internet of Things, Big Data and Security, IoTBDS 2024 DOI http://dx.doi.org/10.5220/0012703400003705. This work presents the LADE proposal, a host-based detection system for EDAs detection.
